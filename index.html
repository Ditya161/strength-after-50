Good to know

A few things you may be wondering.

Still have a question? The programme team can help you decide whether this is a good next step.

Do I need barbell experience?

No. The programme is beginner-friendly and introduces the movements with coaching and options for your starting level.

What if I have not exercised in a while?

The starting-block guide helps you reflect on your experience and the support you prefer. A coach can discuss the programme with you before you decide.

Is a DEXA scan a diagnosis?

No. It is a measurement, not a diagnosis. Talk with your healthcare professional about medical questions or whether a scan is appropriate for you.

What if I have an injury or health condition?

Please speak with your healthcare professional and the programme team before taking part. This guide does not assess medical readiness.

Can I help a parent decide?

Yes. You can explore the guide together; answers should reflect the participant’s own goals, comfort and preferences.

How much does the programme cost?

The confirmed programme price and whether the DEXA scan is included will be shown before booking.
